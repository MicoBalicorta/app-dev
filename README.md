## My Favorite K-Drama

![hwarang](hwarang.webp)
Hwarang: The Poet Warrior Youth is a popular 2016 historical coming-of-age K-drama centered around an elite group of young male warriors in the ancient Silla Kingdom. The characters are celebrated for their distinct personalities, sharp visual styles, and intense bromance.

The Core Trio

• Moo-myung / Kim Sun-woo (played by Park Seo-joon): A gritty, lowborn outsider who rises above his station. Known as "Dog-Bird" due to his unpredictable, untamed nature, he assumes the identity of his deceased best friend, Sun-woo, to protect his friend's sister and uncover the truth behind his death.

• Kim Ah-ro (played by Go Ara): A cheerful, resilient, and hardworking woman of mixed social status (half-sacred bone). She works multiple jobs, primarily as a storyteller and a skilled physician for the Hwarang, finding herself caught in a love triangle between Moo-myung and Sammaekjong.

• Sammaekjong / King Jinheung / Kim Ji-dwi (played by Park Hyung-sik): Silla’s hidden, faceless heir to the throne. Forced into hiding by his overly protective mother to escape assassins, he secretly joins the Hwarang under the alias "Ji-dwi" to build his own strength and rightfully reclaim his crown.
